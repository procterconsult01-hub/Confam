    Fake alert apps

    Scammers use apps that send an SMS or notification that looks exactly like a real credit alert, with your name, the amount and even a balance.

    Ignore the alert. Open your OPay app and check Transaction History and your balance.

    Edited screenshots and receipt generators

    A "transfer successful" receipt only shows what the sender's screen says. It can be edited in seconds or made on a fake receipt website.

    A receipt is never payment. Only your own balance is.

    "It's pending, it will reflect"

    The buyer says the network is slow and the money will land soon, then leaves with the goods.

    No credit, no goods. Let them wait, or come back when it lands.

    Overpayment and "please refund me"

    A fake alert shows more than the price. The buyer asks you to send back the difference, which is your real money.

    Refund nothing until the full amount is in your balance, then refund only to the same account it came from.

    Links to "confirm your payment"

    A message says to click a link or log in to receive the money. The page steals your login and PIN.

    Never log in through a link. OPay will never need your PIN to credit you.

    Busy-shop distraction

    They pay when the shop is full, flash a phone at you, and rush out while you serve others.

    Make "check the app first" a shop rule for every staff member, no matter how busy.

    Real money from a stolen account

    The money is real but came from a hacked account, and can be frozen or reversed later when the owner reports it.

    For big sales, check that the sender name matches the buyer, and keep their phone number and ID on record.

    What to say when a buyer pushes

    "No problem. As soon as it shows in my app, the goods are yours."
    "Our shop rule: we release only after the money lands. Same rule for everybody."
    "If it's pending, you can sit down. I'll refresh my app for you."

    An honest buyer waits. A scammer argues, rushes or leaves.

    Shop rule: no goods before real money

    1. Write the price and your balance before the sale.
    2. Never trust an SMS, screenshot or the buyer's phone.
    3. Open your own OPay app. Find the credit in Transaction History.
    4. Status must say Successful. Balance must go up by the full price.
    5. Pending means wait. Overpayment means refund nothing yet.
